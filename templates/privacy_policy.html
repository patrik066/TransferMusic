Privacy Policy

Introduction

Welcome to TransferMusic! This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data when you use our web application to transfer playlists between Spotify and YouTube.

What Data We Collect

We access your Spotify and YouTube playlist data through OAuth authentication when you log in. This includes playlist names, song titles, and artists necessary for the transfer process.

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to transfer playlists between Spotify and YouTube. We do not store your data permanently—it’s only kept in your session and deleted when you log out.

Data Sharing

We do not share your data with third parties, except as required by Spotify and YouTube APIs to perform the transfer.

Data Security

We use standard security measures to protect your data during the transfer process. However, no online service is 100% secure.

Your Rights

You can revoke our access to your Spotify or YouTube accounts anytime through their settings. No data is retained after you log out.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy, email us at help.transfer.music@gmail.com.
